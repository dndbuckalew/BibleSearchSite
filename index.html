AI Bible Study Companion

Explore, compare, and deepen your understanding of Scripture through AI-powered insight.

About

The AI Bible Study Companion helps users study the Bible more deeply by automatically finding related verses, contextual references, and offering optional insights from theologians. This intelligent system uses OpenAI’s GPT technology to interpret meaning, structure study paths, and engage users interactively.

Key Features

  • Find corresponding Bible verses instantly
  • Compare scripture meaning across contexts
  • Interactive Q&A with AI-driven theological references
  • Supports personal or group Bible study
